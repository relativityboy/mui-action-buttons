01 / CLARITY

Make the wait visible.

People shouldn’t have to wonder whether their click worked. Pending icons and success or error colors give the action a visible rhythm.

02 / LESS PLUMBING

Let the promise do the talking.

Await your action in one callback. The component handles its feedback and ignores extra clicks on that button while the request runs.

03 / A FAMILIAR FIT

Keep the MUI you know.

Your theme, your icons, your button props. Add async feedback without inventing another button system or another styling language.

Small addition. Better feeling.

A promise is all it takes.

Import the stylesheet, pass an async action, and give it a good name. Resolve for success; throw for a dismissible error.
